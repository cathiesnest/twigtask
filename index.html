About TwigTask

Your Business, Better Organized.

What is TwigTask?

TwigTask is a simple digital organizer designed for startups, small businesses, freelancers, service providers, online shops, real estate professionals, and growing teams.

Instead of trying to become a complicated CRM, TwigTask focuses on the everyday things a small business needs to remember: who you are talking to, what opportunities are active, what needs to be done, and what the next move should be.

How TwigTask works

Capture Keep important contacts, leads, and business information in one place.
Organize Give everyday business information a clear home.
Communicate Know who needs a conversation, response, or follow-up.
Follow Up Turn loose reminders into visible tasks and actions.
Grow Use clearer information to make better-informed business decisions.
